Txt

A minimal, distraction-free editor for .txt and Markdown files on iPhone and iPad.

  • Open and edit files in place: on your device, in iCloud Drive or other file providers
  • Find & replace, a live line/column readout, and a coding-friendly keyboard row
  • Markdown preview, word/character/line stats, and export as .txt, .md or .csv
  • No accounts, no tracking, no data collection
